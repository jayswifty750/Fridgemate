Features Deep Dive

Master your kitchen in
3 simple steps

FridgeMate takes the guesswork out of grocery management. Here is exactly how we help you save time, money, and food.

1

Effortless Scanning

Forget manual data entry. Our advanced barcode scanner identifies products instantly. Just point your camera, and FridgeMate adds the item to your digital inventory.

  • Works offline for previously scanned items
  • Manual entry option for fresh produce
2

Smart Expiration Tracking

We estimate expiration dates based on product type and typical shelf life. You can adjust these dates manually, and we'll send you a friendly nudge when it's time to eat.

  • Push notifications 2 days before expiry
  • Track your waste reduction stats
3

AI Recipe Suggestions

Don't know what to cook? FridgeMate looks at your inventory and suggests recipes that use up your ingredients before they go bad.

  • Filters for dietary restrictions
  • Generate shopping lists for missing items
  • Save your favorite meals
