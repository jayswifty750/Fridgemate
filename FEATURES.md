# FridgeMate – Features

FridgeMate is a mobile application designed to help users manage their groceries, track expiration dates, and reduce food waste.

## Core Features

### 1. Barcode Scanning
- Scan product barcodes to help identify grocery items.
- Simplify the process of adding products to your inventory.

### 2. Grocery Inventory Management
- Add grocery items to your personal inventory.
- Keep track of stored food products.
- Delete items when they are no longer needed.

### 3. Expiration Date Tracking
- Monitor expiration dates for grocery items.
- Keep track of which products need attention.

### 4. Color-Coded Expiration Indicators
FridgeMate uses color indicators to help users understand the status of their groceries:

- **Red:** Items that are close to expiring.
- **Orange:** Items that are approaching their expiration dates.
- **Green:** Items that are still within their expiration period.

### 5. Notifications
- Receive notifications related to grocery expiration dates.
- Stay informed about items that may need attention.

### 6. User Interface
- Simple and accessible mobile interface.
- Dedicated screens for managing groceries and navigating app features.
- Visual design created to make grocery tracking easier.
